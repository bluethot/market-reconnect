Slide 2 — The Crisis

The Crisis

A story that repeats across communities — regardless of the trigger.


Imagine a farming community. The harvest is ready. Then crisis strikes.

Whether caused by insecurity, flooding, conflict, road closures, or climate-related disasters — the result is often the same.

Markets stop functioning. Food stops moving. Families lose income.

Common Crisis Triggers

🛣️ Road closures & infrastructure damage
🌡️ Climate-related disasters

Our Solution

The Market Reconnection Platform becomes the coordination center — connecting everyone through one system.

👨‍🌾 Farmers
Register available produce — get matched with buyers even when traditional markets are inaccessible.
